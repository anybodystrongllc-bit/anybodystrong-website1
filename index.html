Why We're Building This

The Premier Adaptive Functional Fitness Center

Very few gyms combine evidence-based behavioral support with real strength and conditioning. We're building the ones that do.

Our mission is to empower neurodivergent athletes through functional fitness — building confidence, independence, social skills, emotional regulation, and lifelong health, one session at a time.

This isn't a fitness gimmick bolted onto a therapy practice, or a therapy program bolted onto a gym. It's both disciplines, built together from day one, coached by people credentialed in each.

This becomes much bigger than fitness. It becomes a place families trust.

What We'll Offer

Programs

Three programs, one coaching standard.

Group

Functional Fitness Classes

Coached group classes built around varied functional movement — strength, gymnastics, and conditioning, scaled to whoever shows up that day.

1-on-1

Personal Training

One-on-one coaching for members who want focused technique work, a specific goal, or a schedule the group classes don't cover.

Adaptive

Adaptive Athlete Program

In-house sessions for neurodivergent athletes, led by a credentialed BCBA alongside our strength coaches — functional fitness and behavioral support in the same session, not two separate appointments.

IN DEVELOPMENT
